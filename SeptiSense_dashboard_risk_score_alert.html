About this prototype

Data flow: physiological sensors → ESP32/ESP8266 node (Wi-Fi) → laptop backend and ML model → this dashboard. The risk score comes from a respiration-free CatBoost model trained on a public clinical dataset and shows the probability of a sepsis-related outcome within the stated horizon. This is an educational and research prototype. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic system and must not be used for patient care.
